How an order runs

From message to finished print

Every order follows the same four stages, so you always know where yours is.

Share the idea

WhatsApp a photo, sketch or STL file with the size you want and how many pieces.

Model & quote

You get a 3D preview and a fixed price covering material, print time and finishing.

Print

Printed in your chosen colour and material, with a photo sent once it comes off the bed.

Finish & ship

Supports removed, edges cleaned, packed and dispatched anywhere in India.
