# LedgerPeak launch plan

Facts below were checked on 19/10/2026 from the sources named. Most are secondary sources, so confirm each one on GOV.UK, the ICO or AAT before you act. Victor and Theo keep this file current.

## Stage 0: before anything else (this week)

1. Read your day-job employment contract for second-job, conflict-of-interest and confidentiality clauses. If it says you must tell your employer, do that first. Never take a client, a colleague's family member or a supplier from your day job unless your contract and employer clearly allow it.
2. Decide the legal form. Sole trader is simplest to start. Theo and Victor can compare it with a limited company, but the tax choice is advice for a qualified accountant.
3. Register as self-employed for Self Assessment on GOV.UK ("Register for Self Assessment"). The rule is to register by 5 October after the end of the tax year you start trading, so trading from now (2026/27) means by 05/10/2027. Register early: the UTR and activation code come by post and can take weeks. (Sources: LITRG and secondary guides, checked 19/10/2026.)

## Stage 1: legally ready to trade (weeks 1 to 4)

4. Anti-money laundering supervision. Bookkeepers must be supervised before giving services. As an AAT student you are not AAT-supervised, so this is normally HMRC. Fees reported from 01/12/2025: premises £400, fit and proper test £500, application fee £300 (waived for small businesses). Secondary source, verify on GOV.UK. The government has announced supervision reform, so ask Victor ("legal: who supervises bookkeepers for AML now?").
5. ICO data protection fee. Tier 1 reported as £52 from February 2025. Secondary source, verify on ico.org.uk.
6. Professional indemnity insurance. Get three quotes for bookkeeper policies (checklist in the policies file).
7. Business bank account, separate from personal.
8. Solicitor check of the engagement letter, terms of business and privacy notice (all drafted, marked DRAFT).
9. AML training, logged in the training log. Adopt the AML policy and firm-wide risk assessment.
10. HMRC agent services account, so you can act for clients once they authorise you.
11. Software partner accounts. Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent run partner programmes for bookkeepers. Theo checks current terms before you sign up.

## AAT: what you can and cannot say

- While you are a student you can provide bookkeeping services, but you must not advertise any connection with AAT, and AAT does not regulate you. (AAT staff forum comment; older post, confirm with AAT.)
- After the Level 3 bookkeeping or accounting qualification you can apply for AAT bookkeeping membership (AATQB) and then a licence, which needs relevant work experience. (aat.org.uk "Be self-employed", checked 19/10/2026.)
- Marketing always says "bookkeeper". Never "accountant", "chartered" or "certified".

12. Google Business Profile for Harrow, a one-page website (Charlotte drafts the copy), and a professional email address.
13. First three clients from your own network (friends' businesses, local trades), never day-job contacts. Offer the DEMO-tested process at the Starter or MTD Ready price.
