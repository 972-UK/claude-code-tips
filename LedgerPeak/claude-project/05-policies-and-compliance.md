# Anti-money laundering policy and firm-wide risk assessment

DRAFT, check with a solicitor.  Owner: Parth Prajapati (MLRO and nominated officer).  Draft date: 19/10/2026.

Legal basis: Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 (as amended); Proceeds of Crime Act 2002; Terrorism Act 2000. Source: legislation.gov.uk, checked 19/10/2026.

## 1. Supervision
LedgerPeak must be registered with an AML supervisor before providing bookkeeping services. Reported HMRC fees from 01/12/2025: premises fee £400, fit and proper test £500, application fee £300 (waived for small businesses) (secondary source, figsflow.com, checked 19/10/2026; I am not sure these are current, verify on GOV.UK "Register or renew your money laundering supervision with HMRC"). The government has announced reform of AML supervision for professional services; check GOV.UK for who supervises bookkeepers now.

## 2. Firm-wide risk assessment
| Risk area | Our exposure | Rating | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clients | Cash-heavy trades (cafés, shops), landlords | Medium | CDD, EDD for cash-heavy, annual reviews |
| Services | Bookkeeping, VAT and MTD preparation; no client money handled | Low | No client money account |
| Geography | Mainly Harrow / NW London | Low | Extra checks on overseas links |
| Delivery | Some remote onboarding | Medium | Video ID check, electronic verification |
| Transactions | Unusual cash deposits, round sums | Medium | Bookkeeper flags to MLRO |
Review: annually or when the business changes.

## 3. Policies
- CDD on every client before work starts (see customer-due-diligence-procedure.md).
- Enhanced due diligence for high risk, PEPs, high-risk third countries.
- Ongoing monitoring; reviews per risk rating.
- Records kept 5 years after the relationship ends.
- Training logged in compliance/registers/training-log.md annually.
- Suspicions reported to the MLRO; SARs via NCA (see suspicious-activity-reporting.md). No tipping off.

# Customer due diligence procedure

DRAFT, check with a solicitor.

1. Identify the client: full name, date of birth, address. Verify with photo ID and proof of address (or reliable electronic verification).
2. Record only that the check was done and where evidence is held: "ID checked, see paper/secure file". Never type document numbers into LedgerPeak files.
3. Companies: Companies House record, directors, beneficial owners over 25%; verify beneficial owners. Check the PSC register and report material discrepancies as required.
4. Understand the nature and purpose of the relationship and source of funds.
5. Screen against the UK sanctions list and for PEP status.
6. Rate the risk (low, medium, high) with reasons in 02-aml-due-diligence.md.
7. Enhanced due diligence if high risk: senior approval (Parth), source of wealth evidence, more frequent review.
8. Do not start work until CDD is complete.
9. Ongoing: review at risk-based intervals and on any trigger event.

# Data breach procedure

DRAFT, check with a solicitor.

1. Contain: stop the breach (revoke access, recall email, change passwords).
2. Record in compliance/registers/data-breach-log.md: date, what happened, data involved, people affected.
3. Assess risk to individuals.
4. If it is likely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms, report to the ICO without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware (UK GDPR Article 33; ico.org.uk, checked 19/10/2026).
5. If high risk, tell the affected individuals without undue delay.
6. Review and fix the cause. Consider speaking to a solicitor and the insurer.

# Suspicious activity reporting procedure

DRAFT, check with a solicitor.

1. Any agent or person who knows or suspects money laundering tells the MLRO (Parth) immediately. Agents stop work on the item and write a note in compliance/registers/suspicious-activity-log.md (internal reference only, no client-facing wording).
2. Do not tell the client or anyone else (tipping off is an offence).
3. MLRO considers the report, records the decision and reasons.
4. If a SAR is needed, MLRO submits it to the National Crime Agency through the NCA SAR Portal. If consent (a DAML) is needed before continuing work, do not proceed until it is received or the notice period ends. Check current NCA guidance on timescales (I am not sure of current figures).
5. Keep records for 5 years.
6. If unsure, take advice from the AML supervisor helpline or a solicitor.
