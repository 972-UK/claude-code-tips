# The first 30 evenings: starting LedgerPeak without registering

Pre-registration mode is ON. Each evening is 45 to 90 minutes. Type the shortcut shown into the LedgerPeak Project (or the slash command in Claude Code). Tick the roadmap as you go.

## Week 1: foundations
| Evening | Type this | What the team does | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | roadmap | Ava and Eleanor walk you through the road to launch | Read your employment contract's second-job clauses (roadmap 1.1) |
| 2 | practice: DEMO Harrow Hill Café | Full practice month: Leo books it, James summarises, Harriet reviews | Redo three of Leo's double entries yourself |
| 3 | study | Iris teaches double entry using the café | Answer the five questions |

| 5 | finance: start-up budget and 12-month cash flow | Edward refines the budget and forecast | Decide your monthly time limit |
