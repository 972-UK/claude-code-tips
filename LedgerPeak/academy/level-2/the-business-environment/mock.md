# The Business Environment: mock

Time limit: 45 minutes. Pass mark 70% (7 of 10). Practice only; real AAT assessments are computer-based with their own timings, check aat.org.uk.

1. A client offers Parth a gift to ignore missing receipts. Which principle is threatened?
2. Who should staff report a money laundering suspicion to?
3. Name the four essential elements of a contract mentioned in this unit.
4. What does inflation mean?
5. Is an invitation to treat (e.g. price label) an offer?
6. Why might a bookkeeper decline work outside their competence?
7. Name one advantage of a limited company over a sole trader.
8. How can higher interest rates affect a small business?
9. A partnership without an agreement: how are profits normally shared?
10. Give one example of a sustainable practice for LedgerPeak.

---

## Mark scheme (iris-bell only, do not peek)

1. Integrity (and objectivity).
2. The firm's nominated officer (MLRO).
3. Offer, acceptance, consideration, intention to create legal relations.
4. A general rise in prices over time, reducing the purchasing power of money.
5. No. It invites customers to make an offer.
6. Professional competence and due care.
7. Limited liability (or separate legal entity / easier to raise capital).
8. Higher borrowing costs and possibly lower customer spending.
9. Equally (Partnership Act 1890 default; verify with current guidance).
10. For example paperless records, reducing travel with video meetings.
