# Introduction to Bookkeeping: mock

Time limit: 45 minutes. Pass mark 70% (7 of 10). Practice only; real AAT assessments are computer-based with their own timings, check aat.org.uk.

1. Net £350, VAT 20%. What is the VAT and gross?
2. List price £800, trade discount 15%, VAT 20%. Calculate net, VAT and gross.
3. Owner pays £5,000 of personal savings into the business bank. Double entry?
4. Owner takes £300 from the business bank for personal use. Double entry?
5. A customer pays £240 owed. Double entry in general ledger?
6. Petty cash float £150; vouchers total £63.40. How much is the top-up?
7. Debit side total £2,400, credit side total £1,750. What is the balance c/d and on which side is it written?
8. Sales returns of £120 gross (£100 net, £20 VAT) from a credit customer. General ledger entries?
9. Does a trial balance that agrees prove there are no errors? Give one reason.
10. Purchase of van fuel £60 by debit card. Capital or revenue, and double entry?

## Mark scheme (iris-bell only, do not peek)

1. VAT £70, gross £420.
2. Discount £120, net £680, VAT £136, gross £816.
3. Dr Bank £5,000; Cr Capital £5,000.
4. Dr Drawings £300; Cr Bank £300.
5. Dr Bank £240; Cr Sales ledger control £240.
6. £63.40.
7. £650 written on the credit side as balance c/d; it is brought down as a debit balance b/d.
8. Dr Sales returns £100; Dr VAT control £20; Cr Sales ledger control £120.
9. No. For example, an error of omission (transaction not recorded at all) does not affect agreement.
10. Revenue. Dr Motor expenses £60; Cr Bank £60.
