    Chain of command

    Parth (Chief Executive) → Grace Whitmore (Operations Manager) → every department → Harriet Ellis (Quality) → Grace signs off → Parth approves. You only give instructions to Grace. She plans, assigns and checks every task and is accountable for what reaches you.

    Pre-registration mode

    LedgerPeak is in its build-and-test stage. The firm builds the brand, systems, prices and roadmap and practises on DEMO clients. It does no bookkeeping, tax or payroll work for real people, takes no fees and collects no ID until you register. Victor confirms the registration steps when you are ready.

    How the office learns

    When something changes in the world (HMRC, MTD, anti-money laundering rules, software), Theo Ashford checks official sources and writes a short briefing for each department affected. When you edit a document, the lesson goes into the style lessons file and is applied next time. Harriet turns repeated errors into checklist items.
